## Non-Recurring Budget Expenses (2026-08-10)

Status: Done. Every budget expense account was previously assumed to recur evenly every month (annual amount / 12), which understated near-term budgeted spend in the month an occasional expense (e.g. an annual insurance premium, a one-off program cost) actually lands, and overstated it in every other month — diluting the accuracy of the 30-60 day cash forecast in `financial-overview.html`.

- **`budget-vs-actual.html`**: Setup Budget's expense rows gained a per-account "🔁 Recurring" / "📌 One-time" selector; picking One-time reveals a month dropdown. State is stored as a new `expenseSchedule` map on the same `budgets/{year}` doc (`{ [accountKey]: { recurring: false, month: 0-11 } }` — absent entry or `recurring !== false` still means the old flat-recurring behavior, so existing budgets need no migration). `delAcc`/`renameAccount` keep the schedule entry in sync when an account is deleted/renamed. Expense BvA's monthly "Period" column, the accumulated Budget Utilization/Efficiency KPIs, and the Monthly View tab's per-month "Target Exp" figure all now sum `expenseBudgetForMonth(key, month)` (full amount in the assigned month, 0 elsewhere for one-time accounts) instead of a flat `annual/12`. Income accounts and annual-period totals are unaffected — annual sums are unchanged either way, only the monthly distribution differs.
- **`financial-overview.html`**: `loadBudgetForForecast()` now also pulls `expenseSchedule`; the 30-60 Day Cash Forecast's "Budgeted pace" expense figure is computed via the same schedule-aware logic, averaged over the two upcoming calendar months (the actual window being forecast) instead of the flat annual/12 split. Income's budgeted pace is unchanged. No other forecast logic, layout, or the real-time listener behavior on this page was touched.

**Extended to income budget accounts (2026-08-12).** The same recurring-vs-one-time distinction now also applies to Setup Budget's income rows, not just expense — an income account (e.g. an annual fund raiser or a one-off donation drive) can be marked one-time and assigned to the month it's actually expected to land.
- **`budget-vs-actual.html`**: `setRecurring`/`setScheduleMonth` (previously expense-only) now take a `type` param and write to a new sibling `incomeSchedule` map via a `scheduleField(type)` helper, mirroring `expenseSchedule` exactly (same absent-entry-means-recurring compatibility, no migration needed). New `isRecurringIncome`/`incomeBudgetForMonth` helpers parallel the expense ones. Income BvA's monthly "Period" column, the accumulated Budget Utilization/Efficiency KPIs, and the Monthly View's per-month "Target Inc" figure all switched from flat `annual/12` to `incomeBudgetForMonth(key, month)`. `delAcc`/`renameAccount` schedule cleanup is now generic across both types instead of expense-only.
- **`financial-overview.html`**: new `budgetedIncomeForMonth(month)` mirrors `budgetedExpenseForMonth`; the 30-60 Day Cash Forecast's "Budgeted pace" income figure now also averages the two upcoming calendar months via the schedule instead of a flat annual/12 split (previously only the expense side got this treatment). The now-unused flat-divide `sumBudgetCategories()` helper was removed.

**Projected Cash Level (Section 15) forecast, especially level-change alerts (2026-08-12).** Section 15's cash monitoring only ever showed the *current* color level (Green/Yellow/Red/Black); the existing 30-60 Day Cash Forecast projected peso amounts but never projected the *level* itself, so a Treasurer had no way to see a level change (e.g. Green sliding toward Yellow) coming before it actually happened.
- **`financial-overview.html`**: new `getForecastedCashLevel(snap, forecast, monthsAhead)` projects Available Operating Cash forward by the same conservative (worst-of actual-vs-budgeted) monthly net already used for the peso forecast, divides by Essential Monthly Operating Expense, and runs it back through the existing `getCashLevel()` thresholds — so "+30 days" and "+60 days" get their own Green/Yellow/Red/Black badge, not just a peso figure.
- The Cash Control card's mini forecast box (`renderCashForecast`) now generates on every render regardless of current level (previously hidden entirely at Green, on the theory only Yellow+ needed the SOP-required 30-60 day forecast) — it now always shows Now/+30-day/+60-day level badges, plus a prominent left-border alert callout whenever the projected level differs from the current one (red-bordered if the change lands within 30 days, amber if only by 60 days).
- The full forecast modal (`renderCashForecastModalBody`) gained a matching "Projected Cash Level (Section 15)" section with the same badges and change alert, right below the existing peso-based 30/60-day projection, or a green "no level change projected" line when the trend holds steady.
- Purely additive: only new pure functions consuming the already-computed `snap`/`forecast` objects and new rendered HTML. The `income`/`expenses` real-time `onSnapshot` listeners that CLAUDE.md calls out as this page's deliberate real-time exception were not touched.

## Cash on Hand / Cash in Bank Mismatch Audit

Status: Done (code side). Triggered by a live report: "my cash on hand and bank does not match the actual record." Audited every write path into the `income`/`expenses` collections across `financial-overview.html`, `tithe-entry.html`, `special-projects.html`, and `expenses.html` for the two bug classes already found and fixed once this session (missing fund-routing field, non-atomic multi-document writes) plus sign errors and orphaned delete cascades. `connectional-ministries.html`/`report-workflow.html` confirmed to not touch `income`/`expenses` at all.

- **`financial-overview.html` `transferFunds()` — non-atomic dual write (highest-confidence direct cause).** The manual "Transfer Funds Between Accounts" feature did two separate sequential `.add()` calls — one `expenses` doc to deduct the source fund, one `income` doc to credit the destination fund — with no batch/transaction. A failure between the two writes (closed tab, network blip) permanently deducts one fund without ever crediting the other, which is exactly the "cash on hand and bank don't match" symptom. Fixed with `db.batch()` so both writes commit together or not at all; also added the missing `postedAt` field to both docs (same ledger-visibility bug class fixed earlier in `liquidation-reimbursements.html` — without it a manual transfer's expense side is invisible in `expenses.html`'s ledger).
- **`tithe-entry.html` `mapTypeToAccount()` — payment method ignored for fund routing (second highest-confidence cause).** This is the main income entry point (single + bulk review, both go through `submitAllEntries`). The form captures a `payment` of `'Cash'` or `'Bank Transfer'` per entry, but `targetAccount` was computed purely from the offering `type` via a hardcoded map that sent `'Source of Funds'`/`'Special Offering'`/`'Occasional Offering'` to `cash-hand` unconditionally — so every regular tithe/offering marked "Bank Transfer" was still booked to Cash on Hand, never Cash in Bank. Fixed to remove each item from the draft array as it individually succeeds, so a retry after a partial failure only resubmits what's actually left.

This was flagged as a candidate bug during the audit, but per explicit direction that logic is intentional/correct as designed — **not** a bug, no change made. Noted here so a future pass doesn't re-flag it.
- **Investigated, no bug found:** `isEffectiveAsOfToday`/date-fallback logic (not a null-exclusion risk); `recordDeductionPayment()` (correctly sets `targetAccId`/`postedAt`); the `hasNetPayroll`/deduction-payment double-counting guard in `financial-overview.html`'s expense listener (already fixed in a prior pass — net-pay cash impact is scoped to the life of the payroll record, not the calendar month, so it does not re-double-count once a deduction-payment doc is later recorded).
